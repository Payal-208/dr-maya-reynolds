===========================================================

PALETTE — "Golden Hour Calm"
  Primary    #4A3F6B dusk plum   (buttons, links, wordmark, footer bar)
             #3B3354 night dusk  (dark quote band)
  Secondary  #EFDDD3 blush clay  ("How therapy works" section)
             #EFEAF4 lavender mist (hero, intro, call-to-action sections)
  Accent     #B5563A terracotta  (emphasis words, check marks, hover states)
             #E8A87C apricot     (links on dark backgrounds)
  Neutrals   #FBF7F2 ivory (page background / text on dark)   #2E2A3B ink (body text)

CONTRAST (WCAG)
  ink on ivory 13.0:1 | plum on ivory 8.9:1 | ivory on night dusk 11.0:1
  terracotta on ivory 4.5:1 (AA) | ink on blush 10.6:1 | ink on lavender mist ~10.5:1
  Terracotta text appears only on ivory (heading emphasis words), never on blush.

LIGHT / DARK RULE (unchanged from the original page)
  light background -> dark ink text;  dark background -> ivory text.

  public/original-assets/custom.css  hard-coded check marks / nav underline / button underline recolored
  public/original-assets/dr-maya-reynolds-wordmark.svg   recolored (plum + terracotta)
  src/app/globals.css, tailwind.config.ts   tokens updated to the new palette
  src/lib/site-config.ts             theme.css added to the stylesheet list (must stay last)
